About

Science for a Changing Ocean is an open platform for exploring ocean science, research, data, tools and scientific knowledge.

The website brings together oceanographic resources, R codes, mapping tools, research notes and educational material related to our changing ocean.

The aim is to make scientific information and useful tools more accessible to students, researchers and anyone interested in understanding the ocean.
